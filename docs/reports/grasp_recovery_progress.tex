{\LARGE\bfseries Grasp Recovery Progress Summary}\par
{\color{accent}\rule{\textwidth}{0.8pt}}
\textbf{Project:} Agent-aware supervision for a frozen RoboTwin VLA \hfill
\textbf{Snapshot:} 12 November 2026

\heading{Objective}

The work validates a VLA-first recovery path for failed mug grasps by either
arm. The frozen Neotix policy remains responsible for normal contact-rich
execution. Deterministic skills intervene only after a verified miss, place the
robot in a safe retry state, and then return control to a fresh VLA invocation.
No policy retraining or arbitrary 14-DoF planner output is introduced.

\heading{Current recovery contract}

\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.94\textwidth}{
Verified failed grasp $\rightarrow$ discard stale VLA actions $\rightarrow$
ground \code{mug\_recovery\_clearance} from the current mug pose $\rightarrow$
execute collision-aware \code{MOVE\_TO} $\rightarrow$ verify endpoint $\rightarrow$
require \code{release\_ready=true} and \code{mug\_toppled=false} $\rightarrow$
open the cleared gripper $\rightarrow$ reset VLA history $\rightarrow$ fresh monitored VLA retry.
}}
\end{center}

The same state machine is used for left-arm acquisition and right-arm pickup.
No arm locking or wrist-rotation heuristic is used. A toppled mug or confirmed
workspace loss terminates the episode instead of attempting an unsafe retry.

\heading{Implemented work}

\begin{tabular}{p{0.27\textwidth}p{0.67\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Area} & \textbf{Implementation} \\
\midrule
Failure monitoring & Event-based failed-grasp monitoring distinguishes inherited analytic contact from a new VLA closure attempt, avoiding premature retry failure. \\
Chunk handling & Remaining policy actions are discarded at intervention; every retry starts with reset policy history and fresh observations. \\
Grounded clearance & A symbolic pose is recomputed above the current mug from RGB-D/development evidence rather than using a hard-coded world pose. \\
Native motion & Recovery uses RoboTwin/CuRobo planning, measured TCP state, embodiment transforms, bounded waypoints, and native qpos execution. \\
Retry normalization & Clearance must complete within measured position/orientation tolerances before a support-gated gripper opening and VLA handback. \\
Evidence separation & Public perception remains planner-facing; simulator privilege is explicitly development-only and fills experimental evidence gaps. \\
Reproducibility & State-checked prefix replay, structured skill traces, 30 FPS two-camera video, and bounded T4 Modal configurations support repeatable experiments. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\heading{Evidence so far}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The vertical-clearance replay completed two recovery moves in 28 physics
  ticks each, with endpoint errors of 0.33 mm and 0.30 mm and orientation errors
  below 0.001 rad.
  \item The later grounded-waypoint replay completed both retry moves with
  endpoint errors of 0.02 mm and 0.65 mm. This confirmed the corrected TCP/end-link
  transform and the multi-waypoint path.
  \item The event monitor gave the VLA genuine post-recovery attempts instead of
  immediately classifying inherited contact loss as a new failed grasp.
  \item In the normalized live replay, the initial miss stopped after 77 policy
  actions and discarded three stale actions. Clearance finished in 78 physics
  ticks with 0.30 mm position error; the gripper then opened from 0.0 to 1.0.
  Fresh VLA inference confirmed a right-arm lift after 68 further actions.
  \item The complete CPU suite currently passes: 696 tests covering planner
  sequencing, skill contracts, guarded opening, perception, and left/right retry behavior.
\end{itemize}

\heading{Remaining gaps}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Repeat the successful sequence across left and right failures and multiple seeds;
  report retry success, abort causes, endpoint error, and policy actions to recovery.
  \item Replace remaining development-oracle evidence incrementally as public RGB-D
  predicates demonstrate equivalent reliability, especially support, toppling,
  workspace presence, and arm-specific lift.
  \item Replay the verified contract through the Opus supervisor, ensuring its
  prompts select recovery only after an evidenced miss and never bypass the gates.
  \item Validate hanging recovery separately; it is not established by grasp-retry success.
\end{enumerate}

\heading{Current conclusion}

The full right-arm recovery contract has now succeeded once in a recorded live
replay: intervention, clearance, guarded opening, fresh inference, and verified
lift. This establishes feasibility, not reliability. The next milestone is a
multi-seed evaluation of both arms using public perception wherever it is reliable.
